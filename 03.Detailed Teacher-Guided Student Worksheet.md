### C. First Contact

Listen / watch / read **without taking detailed notes**.

Your only task:

> **Understand the general topic and message.**

### Student Check

☐ I know the topic.
☐ I understand the general message.
☐ I can identify the main issue.

### Teacher Guidance

**Do not teach vocabulary yet.**

The teacher should:

* establish the topic
* activate background knowledge
* clarify the task
* prevent learners from trying to understand every word

**Teacher question:**

> “What is this mainly about?”

### Teacher Guidance

The teacher checks **comprehension before language analysis**.

If students cannot explain the general message, do not move immediately to detailed analysis.

**Teacher intervention:**

* replay a short section

### Teacher Guidance

Do not give students the answers.

Use prompts such as:

> Why?

> Can you explain that?

> What makes you think so?

> Can you give me an example?

> What would happen if...?

> Is there another side?

> Do you agree?

The teacher's job is to **develop the learner's thinking**, not supply the content.

Check whether the ideas are **connected**.

The learner should not simply have seven unrelated answers.

Look for:

**Idea → Reason → Example → Result → Evaluation**

# 10 · RECONSTRUCT

### 3–5 minutes

Close the source.

You now have to reconstruct the message.

### Step 1 · Keywords

Write only **5–7 keywords**.

---

---

### Step 2 · Retell

Speak for **30–60 seconds**.

Explain the original idea **in your own words**.

### Check

☐ I did not read the source.
☐ I used my own words.
☐ I included the main idea.
☐ I included at least one supporting detail.

### Teacher Guidance

Do not interrupt for every error.

Listen for:

* comprehension
* organisation
* vocabulary access
* fluency

Make brief notes for later feedback.

# THE TEACHING LOGIC

```text
INPUT
  ↓
UNDERSTAND
  ↓
NOTICE
  ↓
EXTRACT
  ↓
SELECT
  ↓
DEVELOP
  ↓
CONNECT
  ↓
RECONSTRUCT
  ↓
SUPPORTED SPEAKING
  ↓
INDEPENDENT SPEAKING
  ↓
DIAGNOSE
  ↓
FEEDBACK
  ↓
REPEAT
  ↓
REDUCE SUPPORT
  ↓
TRANSFER
  ↓
INDEPENDENT COMMUNICATION
```

## Core Principle

> **The teacher does not simply teach the learner what to say.**
>
> **The teacher teaches the learner how to turn input into ideas, ideas into language, and language into independent communication.**
